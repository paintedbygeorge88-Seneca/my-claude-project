## Content overlap warning

Four of the biggest videos cover **the same event** — a walkthrough of Elon Musk's Casita
(Enes Yilmazer, Faze Rug, Graham Stephan, and Orkun Isitmak in Turkish). Expect the same
rooms, reveals and beats across all four. The campaign says *"Vary your clips. Don't post
the same moment 10 times."* — so treat these four as ONE story told four ways, not four
independent sources.

## Language note

Two sources are not in English (Orkun Isitmak — Turkish; 808 — Russian). Captions would

priority for early clips.
