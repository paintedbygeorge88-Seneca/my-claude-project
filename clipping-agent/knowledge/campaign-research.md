### Decision made
Model: **paid clipping campaigns** (clip someone else's content with permission, get paid per 1,000 views).

Scale check: Forbes (April 2026) reported Whop Content Rewards paying $40,000+/day across ~1M videos/month. Clients have included Polymarket, ElevenLabs, Justin Bieber. It's real, not a scam.

Example live Whop campaigns (July 2026 snapshot, for calibration only — these move fast):
Roobet $1/1K with $250K budget; Call of Duty MW4 $1.50/1K with $120K; Boxabl $0.50/1K with $85K; Pacinos $1/1K with $50K.

### The key insight: saturation is per-campaign, not per-niche

Chasing an "unsaturated niche" is the wrong frame. Campaign pools are **first-come-first-served** — views that finish verifying after the budget is exhausted pay nothing. So the thing to avoid is a campaign whose pool is nearly drained, in any niche.

**Selection rule:** multiply CPM by *remaining* budget, not total budget.
A high CPM with a small remaining pool is worth less than a mid CPM with $100K+ left.

### The real edge: format tier, not niche

Whop's own rate guidance:
- ~$1 per 1K — basic clip (cut, caption, post)
- ~$2 per 1K — faceless UGC-style edit
- ~$3.50 per 1K — on-camera UGC

Most clippers compete at the $1 tier because it's the easily automated one. The higher tiers are structurally less crowded because they take more effort. Our pipeline automates the boring part of the $1 tier, which frees time to compete in the $2 tier where fewer people are.

### Why the highest-CPM niches pay so much

Kick gambling campaigns pay $10+/1K specifically because fewer clippers will accept the content and the **account risk**. Crypto and finance sit at $4–6+.
→ High CPM is compensation for ban risk, not free money. Bad first move on a brand-new account with zero trust history.

### Realistic beginner numbers
3 posts/day averaging 3,000 views at $1 CPM ≈ $270/month gross. After a documented 20–30% haircut for budget dry-ups, bans and verification disputes: **~$190–210 in month one.** Treat month one as learning the feedback loop, not earning.

### 2026-08-03 — Dreamina AI: DEAD END (do not join)

Opened the campaign detail. Description confirms clipping is allowed as an equal option
to original UGC ("or clip the provided content"), platforms TikTok / Instagram / YouTube
Shorts, tied to the Seedance 2.0 Mini launch, requires a tracking link in bio.

It also says: "MUST FILL OUT THIS FORM PRIOR TO PARTICIPATING:
https://form.pandadoc.com/form/ZLuTNCLpvwAJZaLmrBrDTN"

**That form returns "This form is no longer accepting responses."**

So the mandatory onboarding step is shut while the campaign is still featured on the
discover carousel advertising a $60,000 budget. You cannot legitimately join.

**Rule learned — verify before valuing:** a campaign being listed, featured, and showing
a big budget does NOT mean it is open. Whop's featured carousel carries stale listings.
Before ranking any campaign, check that its intake is actually open — follow every
external link in the description. Cheapest possible disqualifier, so do it first.

This also means the advertised budgets on the carousel are even less trustworthy than
already flagged: they are neither "remaining" nor proof the campaign is live.
