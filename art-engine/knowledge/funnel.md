# The funnel: from stranger to collector

**Rule: everything here is free. No paid ads, ever.** Every tool is on a free plan
(GitHub Pages, Pinterest, MailerLite/Kit free tier, print-on-demand charges only per sale).

## How we target the right people without ads
Ads target by paying the platform. We target by **putting the work where the right
people already are, and using the words they search with.**

1. **Neighbour accounts.** Find 30–50 accounts whose followers would love this work:
   expressive/figurative painters, poets writing about emotion, therapists and
   mental-health creators, dark-aesthetic and art-curation pages. Spend 10 min a day
   leaving real comments on their new posts. Their followers see you, and the platform
   learns who your work is for.
2. **Collabs.** Instagram "Collab" posts show on both accounts. Pair a painting with a
   poet's verse, or a therapist's caption about masking. You reach their whole audience for free.
3. **Feature pages.** Submit to art curation accounts that repost artists (many are free).
4. **Searchable words.** Instagram and TikTok work like search engines now. On-screen
   text and captions say exactly what it is: "abstract face painting about anxiety".
   Small, specific hashtags (5–8), not huge ones like #art.
5. **Hooks that select the audience.** "If you've ever said 'I'm fine' when you weren't…"
   stops exactly the people who feel this work and scrolls past everyone else. That's targeting.
6. **Pinterest + Google + Reddit.** People searching for emotional or face art find it
   through automated pins and site pages.

Claude builds and refreshes the neighbour list, collab targets and feature pages monthly
(see "run outreach" in CLAUDE.md).

Built from `artist-profile.md`. The work is emotional and personal, so people buy
**the meaning and the artist**, not wall décor. Every stage leads with the story:
*the faces we hide*.

```
  SEEN            →   CURIOUS           →   CONNECTED            →   BUYS
  short videos        website story         email list               print
  Pinterest           "Which mask" quiz     studio letters           commission
  communities         collection pages      DMs                      (original)
  Barcelona IRL
```

## 1. SEEN: reach the people who feel this work

### a) Short process videos: the main engine (you + Claude, ~1.5 h/week)
Watching a face emerge out of abstract chaos is hypnotic, and the subject (hidden
emotions, masks) gets shared because people see themselves in it. Videos reach
strangers. Photos mostly reach existing followers.

- **What to film:** phone on a stand while you paint. No extra time.
  Once a week, a 30–60 s talking clip: the story of one face, what it's hiding, a
  question to viewers ("What's the face you show at work?").
- **Where:** the same clip goes to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- **Claude's job:** pick the best moments, write the hook, captions in English + Spanish,
  hashtags, a pinned comment, and a posting schedule. Same pipeline idea as `clipping-agent/`.
- **Hooks that suit this work:** "This is what anxiety looks like to me" ·
  "Everyone wears a mask. This is mine" · "I painted the face you make when you say
  'I'm fine'" · "Tell me how you feel and I'll paint your inner face."
- **Every caption ends with one call to action:** take the quiz, or "link in bio for prints".

### b) Pinterest: automated (0 h/week)
Already built. The engine now writes pins around **emotional and face-art searches**
("abstract face painting", "emotional art print", "moody expressive portrait") instead
of home-décor terms. It runs every day without you.

### c) Communities where collectors and emotional-art people already are (~20 min/week)
- Reddit: r/Art, r/painting, r/AbstractArt, r/ArtistLounge (follow each subreddit's
  self-promo rules; post the work and story, not links).
- Curated art marketplaces with collector audiences: **Saatchi Art**, **Singulart**,
  **Artfinder**. List the face works there. Collectors browse these to discover artists.
- **Licensing pitch:** these faces suit **album covers, book covers and poetry/mental-health
  podcasts**. Claude finds 10 small musicians, authors and podcasters a month and drafts
  messages (Gmail drafts, you send).

### d) Barcelona in person (once a quarter)
Open studio or pop-up (Poblenou, Gràcia, El Born cafés, Palo Alto Market), expat
groups, art nights. Every in-person contact gets the email QR code.

## 2. CURIOUS: give them a reason to stay

### The "Which mask do you wear?" quiz (to build)
Five or six quick questions about how people feel and what they hide. Each result is
one of your face paintings, with a short paragraph about that emotional state. To see
the full result and get a free phone wallpaper of their painting, they enter their email.
- It turns your theme into something **personal and shareable** ("I got *The Quiet One*").
- It's built-in content: "Comment your result" in videos.
- It sorts buyers by emotion, so emails can recommend the right print.

### Website changes (to build)
- Homepage leads with the story and positioning line, not a grid of products.
- Collections by theme (e.g. *Masks*, *Inner Weather*, *Expressive*).
- Each painting page: the story of that face, prints, "commission your own" link.
- **Commission page:** "Your inner portrait." The client answers a short questionnaire
  about how they feel and what they hide, and you paint their subconscious face.
  Clear tiers and prices, enquiry form.

## 3. CONNECTED: email is the asset you own

Free tool: **MailerLite** (free up to 1,000 subscribers) or **Kit** (free up to 10,000).
Both have embed forms and automated sequences.

Automated welcome sequence (Claude writes it once, it runs forever):
1. **Day 0:** your quiz result + wallpaper + who I am (Barcelona, the dog, why faces)
2. **Day 3:** the story behind the most-loved face piece
3. **Day 7:** prints of your quiz painting, plus a first-order code (10%)
4. **Day 14:** commissions, "what would your inner face look like?"

Then a **monthly studio letter**: new faces, a process clip, one personal note.
Claude drafts it and you approve (10 min).

Also: email the **old shop's past buyers** once with "I'm back + what's new". They're
the warmest people you have.

## 4. BUYS: what they can buy

| Product | Price idea (to confirm) | How it's fulfilled |
|---|---|---|
| Fine art print, A4–A2 | €45–120 | Print on demand (Gelato/Prodigi), ships worldwide |
| Small commission | ~€250–400 | You paint, ship |
| Large commission | €600+ | You paint, ship |

### Rough numbers to hit €1,000/month
e.g. **12 prints (~€55 avg) + 1 small commission (~€350)**.
- At ~2% of subscribers buying per month, that needs a list of roughly **800–1,200 people**.
- At ~25–35% quiz-to-email conversion, that's ~300–400 quiz takers a month,
  which consistent short-video posting can realistically drive within 6–9 months.

## Your week (2–3 h)
| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| While painting | Phone on stand, record | 0 |
| Once | 30–60 s talking clip | 15 min |
| Claude session | Send clips → get edited captions/hooks/schedule, approve | 30 min |
| Spread out | Reply to comments and DMs (this is where sales happen) | 60 min |
| Once | One community post | 20 min |

Monthly (30 min): approve the studio letter, send outreach drafts, check the numbers.

## What we measure
Followers aren't the goal. We track: **quiz takers → subscribers → buyers**, plus
which faces and hooks drive each. Double down on what works every month.
