# Artist profile (from interview, Oct 2026)

Source of truth for positioning, copy and targeting. Update when anything changes.

## The work
- Abstract art of **subconscious minds and faces**. Faces that emerge from expressive,
  abstract mark-making.
- Themes: **inner emotion / mental states** (what people feel but don't show) and
  **identity / the masks we wear** (persona vs. real self).
- Also plays with other genres for **bold, expressive pieces**. These are secondary and
  shouldn't lead the brand.
- One-line positioning (draft, artist to approve):
  *"I paint the faces we hide: abstract portraits of the subconscious."*

## Evidence of who buys
- **The face pieces sold best.**
- **Buyers found the work through Instagram** (DMs and links).
- Has sold originals and prints (old online shop).
- The niche: collectors and art people. Strong reactions, but they don't fit a
  demographic yet. Treat early months as a test to find out who they are.

## Artist
- Based in Barcelona with a dog. 38, painting most of life.
- **Comfortable on camera and talking about the work.** This is the biggest asset.
- Time: **2–3 hours/week** outside painting.
- Budget: €0 for ads.

## The other half: coach and facilitator
- **Men's support coach** and **breathwork + ice bath facilitator**.
- This isn't a distraction from the art. It's the same subject. The paintings show the
  masks and hidden emotions that the coaching helps men take off.
- The audience overlap is the niche: **men doing inner work** (men's circles,
  breathwork, cold exposure, therapy-literate), the people who love them, and the
  coaches, facilitators and retreats in that world.
- The artist is already **inside** that community, which gives free, trusted reach.
