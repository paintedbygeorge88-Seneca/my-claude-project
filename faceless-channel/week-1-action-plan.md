# Week 1 Action Plan — Start Earning with Clipping Campaigns

**Goal:** get your first paid clips live this week, with no audience, no coding, and no legal risk.
**Method:** Whop clipping campaigns (a creator pays you to cut short clips from their long videos and post them). You're given permission, so no copyright problem.
**Created:** 2026-08-02

> Read `research-reference.md` Section 9 first if you haven't — it explains *why* this is the safe, fast option.

---

## Before you start — what you need
- [ ] A phone or laptop (laptop is easier for editing).
- [ ] An email address for signing up.
- [ ] A social account to post clips on (TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram — check which each campaign allows). **Tip:** use a fresh account per niche so your clips aren't mixed with personal stuff.
- [ ] ~£0–15. You can start 100% free with CapCut; a paid cutter just saves time later.

⚠️ **Money rule:** you should never have to *pay to join* a legit clipping campaign. If one asks for a joining fee, skip it.

---

## Day 1 — Set up your accounts (about 1 hour)

1. **Make a Whop account** → go to whop.com, sign up with your email.
2. In Whop, search for **"clipping"** or **"content rewards"**. These are the campaigns that pay per view.
3. **Shortlist 2–3 campaigns** that are:
   - **Active** (recent posts, budget still available — it'll say).
   - In a **niche you find interesting** (gaming, business/podcasts, motivation, comedy — pick what you'd happily watch for hours).
   - Clear on **rules and pay rate** (e.g. "$1 per 1,000 views", which platforms allowed, required hashtags/tags).
4. **Don't clip yet.** Just read each campaign's rulebook fully. Note: allowed platforms, min/max clip length, required captions or account tags, and how they track your views.

📌 *If Whop feels overwhelming, alternatives that run the same kind of campaigns: search "clipping campaigns" — platforms like ClipAffiliates also list them. Whop is the biggest.*

---

## Day 2 — Set up your editing tool (about 1 hour)

Pick ONE to start:
- **Free route: CapCut** (capcut.com or the app). Manual but powerful, and free. Best if you're on a tight budget.
- **Faster route: Ssemble (~$15/mo)** or **quso.ai (free tier)** — these auto-find good moments, add captions, and format to vertical. Worth it once you're posting volume.

**Do this now:**
1. Install/sign up.
2. Watch ONE 5-minute beginner tutorial for your chosen tool (search its name + "beginner tutorial" on YouTube).
3. Make one throwaway test clip from any video so you know the buttons. Don't post it.

**Format basics every clip needs:**
- Vertical **9:16** (1080×1920).
- **Captions/subtitles on** (most people watch on mute).
- A **strong first 1–2 seconds** (the hook) — start on the most interesting moment, not a slow intro.
- 15–60 seconds is the sweet spot for most campaigns.

---

## Day 3–4 — Make and post your first clips (2–3 hours)

For your top campaign:
1. **Get the source video** the campaign points to (the creator's long stream/podcast/video).
2. **Find 5 strong moments.** Look for: a surprising line, a hot take, a funny bit, a "wait what?" moment, or a clear useful tip. If *you'd* stop scrolling for it, it's a candidate.
3. **Cut each into a 15–45s clip**, add captions, and put the best line as a text hook at the top.
4. **Follow the campaign rules exactly** — required tags, hashtags, which account, any watermark/branding. Breaking rules = you don't get paid.
5. **Post them** and **submit the links** back in the campaign so your views get tracked.
6. Post to the allowed platform(s). Space them out (a few hours apart) rather than all at once.

🎯 **Target for the week:** 5–10 clips submitted. This is a learning run — the goal is data, not a fortune.

---

## Day 5–7 — Review what worked (1 hour)

1. Check which clips got the **most views**. That's your signal.
2. Ask: what did the winners have in common? (Topic? Hook line? Length? Moment type?)
3. Make **3–5 more clips** copying what worked.
4. Note your total earnings so far (rate × your views ÷ 1,000). Set expectations: **first week is often £5–50, sometimes less.** That's normal — you're proving the loop works and learning the skill. It scales as you get better at picking moments.

---

## What "good" looks like after Week 1
- ✅ You've joined a campaign and submitted real clips.
- ✅ You can cut, caption and format a vertical clip without stress.
- ✅ You've seen which of your clips got views (even if small).
- ✅ You have a first (small) payout tracking, or a clear view of how it pays.

That's a working, legal income loop with zero audience. **Do NOT jump to building your own faceless channel until this feels easy** — clipping teaches you what goes viral, which is the exact skill the channel needs.
